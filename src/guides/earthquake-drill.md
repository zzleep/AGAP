> **Seismic Profile**: Santa Rosa sits close to the West Valley Fault system in Western Laguna. High preparedness is vital.

## 1. During Shaking — Duck, Cover, Hold
- Drop down on hands and knees so you are not knocked over.
- Take cover under a sturdy table, desk, or reinforced door frame. Protect head and neck with arms.
- Hold on to your shelter until shaking stops completely.

## 2. Evacuation
- Do NOT use elevators. Use fire escape stairwells in high-rises and complexes (Nuvali, Solenad, Technopark offices).
- In Bel-Air, Laguna Bel-Air, Nuvali, and Santa Rosa Estates: assemble in open parks away from walls, electric poles, and glass facades.
- In Laguna Technopark and Greenfield Automotive Park: shut off gas lines and evacuate to company assembly grounds.

## 3. Designated Assembly Grounds
- Santa Rosa City Plaza — Brgy. Kanluran (Poblacion Ward 1)
- Santa Rosa City Hall Grounds — Brgy. Tagapo
- Nuvali Central Park & Solenad Open Fields — Brgy. Don Jose / Santo Domingo
- Barangay Sports Complexes & Open Grounds — All Barangays

## 4. After the Shaking
- Check gas lines for leaks (sniff for LPG odor). Do not switch on electrical circuits if a leak is suspected.
- Inspect walls for diagonal shear cracks before re-entering home.

# Earthquake SOP (Duck, Cover & Hold)

## 1. During Seismic Shaking
- **DUCK**: Drop down onto your hands and knees immediately.
- **COVER**: Cover your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk. If no shelter is nearby, cover your face and head with your arms.
- **HOLD**: Hold on to your shelter until shaking completely stops.

## 2. Post-Shaking Evacuation
- Evacuate building calmly via stairs — **do not use elevators**.
- Proceed to designated open space evacuation assembly points (e.g. City Plaza, Barangay Open Grounds).
- Check for gas leaks, water line ruptures, and structural cracks before re-entering.

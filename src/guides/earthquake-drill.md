# Earthquake & Seismic Safety Guide

> **Seismic Profile**: Santa Rosa City is located in close proximity to the West Valley Fault system in Western Laguna. High preparedness is vital.

## 1. During Seismic Shaking (Immediate Action)
- **DUCK**: Drop down onto your hands and knees immediately to prevent being knocked over.
- **COVER**: Take shelter under a sturdy table, desk, or reinforced door frame. Protect your head and neck with your arms.
- **HOLD ON**: Hold firmly to your shelter until all shaking completely ceases.

## 2. Evacuation from Subdivisions & Workplaces
- **Do NOT Use Elevators**: Use external fire escape stairwells in high-rise buildings and commercial complexes (Nuvali, Solenad, Technopark offices).
- **Subdivision Protocol**: Residents in Bel-Air, Laguna Bel-Air, Nuvali, and Santa Rosa Estates should assemble in central open parks away from concrete perimeter walls, electric poles, and glass facades.
- **Industrial Park Protocol**: Factories in Laguna Technopark and Greenfield Automotive Park should execute automated emergency shutoff for gas lines and evacuate to designated company assembly grounds.

## 3. Designated Open-Space Assembly Grounds in Santa Rosa
- **Santa Rosa City Plaza** — *Brgy. Kanluran (Poblacion Ward 1)*
- **Santa Rosa City Hall Grounds** — *Brgy. Tagapo*
- **Nuvali Central Park & Solenad Open Fields** — *Brgy. Don Jose / Santo Domingo*
- **Barangay Sports Complexes & Open Grounds** — *All Barangays*

## 4. Post-Earthquake Inspection
- Check gas lines for leaks (sniff for LPG odor). Do not turn on electrical switches if gas leak is suspected.
- Inspect walls for structural diagonal shear cracks before re-entering home.

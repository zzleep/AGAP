# Typhoon Emergency & Hotline Guide

> **City Emergency Operations Center**: CDRRMO Santa Rosa maintains 24/7 monitoring during signal alerts across all 18 barangays.

## 1. Pre-Storm Action Checklist (24 Hours Before Landfall)
- **Structural Reinforcement**: Check G.I. sheet roof fasteners, clear gutters, and trim tree limbs near overhead power lines.
- **Water Supply Storage**: Store 4 liters of potable water per person per day for at least 3 days.
- **72-Hour Family Go-Bag**: Pack canned goods, can opener, high-lumens flashlight, spare batteries, battery-powered radio, power banks, first aid kit, and essential prescription medicines.
- **Vehicle Preparation**: Park cars on elevated terrain (e.g. designated city multi-level parking or high ground subdivisions) away from large trees.

## 2. During Typhoon Impact
- **Stay Away From Windows**: Move to interior rooms away from glass windows and doors.
- **Monitor AGAP Live Feed**: Follow real-time announcements from CDRRMO and barangay captains regarding preemptive evacuation.
- **Coastal & River Evacuation**: Residents along Laguna de Bay or Silang-Santa Rosa River must evacuate BEFORE nightfall or rising waters.

## 4. Post-Typhoon Recovery Protocol
- Inspect home electrical wiring before switching on main breaker.
- Do not touch downed power lines or flooded transformers. Report via AGAP Community Intake.

> **City EOC**: CDRRMO Santa Rosa maintains 24/7 monitoring during signal alerts across all 18 barangays.

## 1. Pre-Storm Checklist (24 Hours Before Landfall)
- Check roof fasteners, clear gutters, trim tree limbs near overhead power lines.
- Store 4 liters of potable water per person per day for at least 3 days.
- Pack Go-Bag: canned goods, can opener, flashlight, spare batteries, battery radio, power banks, first aid kit, prescription meds.
- Park cars on elevated ground, away from large trees.

## 2. During the Typhoon
- Stay in interior rooms, away from windows and glass doors.
- Follow CDRRMO and barangay captain announcements on AGAP for preemptive evacuation.
- Evacuate BEFORE nightfall or rising waters if you live along Laguna de Bay or Silang-Santa Rosa River.

## 4. Post-Typhoon Recovery
- Inspect home electrical wiring before switching on the main breaker.
- Do not touch downed power lines or flooded transformers. Report via AGAP Community Intake.

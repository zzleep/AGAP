# Typhoon Preparedness & Evacuation SOP

## 1. Before the Typhoon (Alert Phase)
- **Secure Structures**: Check roofs, gutters, and tree branches near home wiring.
- **Emergency Kit**: Prepare 3 days of clean drinking water (3L/person/day), non-perishable food, flashlight, battery radio, power banks, and essential medicines.
- **Save Hotlines**: Store CDRRMO Santa Rosa `(049) 530-0011` and Emergency Response `911`.

## 2. During the Typhoon (Impact Phase)
- **Stay Indoors**: Move away from glass windows and external doors.
- **Monitor Alerts**: Keep AGAP active for real-time barangay evacuation notices.
- **Rising Water Warning**: If living near Silang-Santa Rosa River or Laguna de Bay tributaries, prepare immediate evacuation before floodwaters reach ankle height.

## 3. After the Typhoon (Recovery Phase)
- Do not enter damaged structures until declared safe by CDRRMO inspectors.
- Report fallen power lines or blocked roads using the AGAP Community Report feature.

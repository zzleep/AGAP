> **Coastal Zone**: For residents, fisherfolk, and boat operators in Aplaya, Sinalhan, and Caingin along the Laguna de Bay shoreline.

## 1. Water Level Monitoring
- When LLDA/CDRRMO lake gauges near warning marks (monsoon rains or storm surge), preemptive evacuation is enforced.
- Strong east or northeast typhoon winds can push lake waters up to 100–200m inland along flat shorelines.

## 2. Fisherfolk & Boat Protocol
- Anchor motorboats and bancas at elevated mangrove or river mouth havens in Brgy. Aplaya.
- Move outboard motors, fishing nets, and fuel tanks to high ground or barangay storage.
- No sailing during PAGASA Gale Warnings or CDRRMO Advisory flags.

## 3. Household Protection
- Store furniture, electronics, and food on raised platforms or second floors.
- Lake floodwater carries raw runoff — keep children out of inundation.
- Move elderly, children, and pregnant women to Aplaya Elementary School or Sinalhan Gym early.

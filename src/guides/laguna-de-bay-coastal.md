# Coastal Inundation & Lake Surge Guide

> **Coastal Zone Focus**: Specifically tailored for residents, fisherfolk, and boat operators in Barangays Aplaya, Sinalhan, and Caingin along the Laguna de Bay shoreline.

## 1. Coastal Water Level Monitoring
- **LLDA & CDRRMO Lake Gauges**: When lake water elevation approaches warning marks due to prolonged monsoon rains or typhoon storm surge, preemptive evacuation is enforced.
- **Seiche & Wind-Driven Surge**: Strong easterly or north-easterly winds during typhoons can push lake waters inland up to 100-200 meters along flat shoreline barangays.

## 2. Fisherfolk & Boat Operator Protocol
- **Secure Vessels Early**: Anchor motorboats and banca fishing vessels securely at elevated mangrove or river mouth havens in Brgy. Aplaya.
- **Remove Outboard Motors**: Transfer outboard motors, fishing nets, and fuel tanks to high ground or barangay storage facilities.
- **No-Sailing Enforcement**: Under no circumstances should fishing vessels venture into Laguna de Bay during PAGASA Gale Warnings or CDRRMO Advisory flags.

## 3. Household Protection along the Shoreline
- **Elevate Living Areas**: Store furniture, electronics, and food stocks on raised wooden platforms or second floors.
- **Waterborne Sanitation**: Floodwaters along lake shorelines harbor raw runoff. Keep children from swimming or playing in lake inundation.
- **Early Evacuation Center Transit**: Move vulnerable family members (elderly, children, pregnant women) to Aplaya Elementary School or Sinalhan Gym early.

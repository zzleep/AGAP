# Flood & River Overflow Guide

> **High-Risk Zones**: Barangays Aplaya, Sinalhan, Caingin, Tagapo, Santo Domingo, Malitlit, Dita, Market Area, and Poblacion.

## 1. Monitoring & Early Warning Triggers
- **Silang-Santa Rosa River Level**: Monitor CDRRMO bridge gauge markers. When water reaches **Orange Level (Warning)**, prepare Go-Bags immediately.
- **Laguna de Bay Spillover**: Coastal barangays (Aplaya, Sinalhan, Caingin) must execute early evacuation when lake surge warnings are announced.
- **AGAP Flow Engine Alert**: Check live route risk on AGAP before departing.

## 2. Household Emergency Actions
- **Electrical Isolation**: Shut down your home main breaker if floodwaters approach wall sockets or floor level.
- **Sanitation Protection**: Elevate clean drinking water containers, non-perishable food, and essential medications above head level.
- **Document Safety**: Secure birth certificates, land titles, and IDs in waterproof sealed pouches.

## 3. Designated Evacuation Centers in Santa Rosa
- **Santa Rosa Multi-Purpose Complex (City Coliseum)** — *Brgy. Macabling*
- **Santa Rosa Central Elementary School** — *Brgy. Poblacion*
- **Balibago Elementary School** — *Brgy. Balibago*
- **Aplaya Elementary School & Covered Court** — *Brgy. Aplaya*
- **Sinalhan Elementary School & Gym** — *Brgy. Sinalhan*
- **Caingin Covered Court** — *Brgy. Caingin*

## 4. Health & Waterborne Hazard Prevention
- **Leptospirosis Risk**: Avoid wading in floodwaters. If contact is unavoidable, wear tall rubber boots and wash legs thoroughly with soap immediately.
- **Boil Water Advisory**: Drink only bottled or boiled water for at least 3 minutes post-flood.

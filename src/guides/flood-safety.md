# Flood Water & Evacuation SOP

## 1. Immediate Household Safety Actions
- **Electrical Safety**: Turn off main circuit breaker if water approaches socket levels.
- **Valuables**: Move electronic equipment, food supplies, and important documents to high shelves or second level.

## 2. Evacuation Protocol
- When CDRRMO issues a **Warning (Orange)** or **Danger (Red)** alert, execute early evacuation along primary green/orange routes.
- **Do NOT Wade in Unknown Waters**: Hidden debris and electrocution hazards exist in moving water.

## 3. Designated Evacuation Centers in Santa Rosa
- **Santa Rosa Multi-Purpose Complex** (Barangay Macabling)
- **Santa Rosa Central Elementary School** (Barangay Poblacion)
- **Balibago Elementary School** (Barangay Balibago)

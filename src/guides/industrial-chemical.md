# Industrial Hazmat & Chemical Leak Guide

> **Industrial Hub Profile**: Santa Rosa houses major industrial complexes including Laguna Technopark (LTIB), Greenfield Automotive Park, and Santa Rosa Commercial Complex.

## 1. Recognizing Chemical & Hazmat Alerts
- **Audible Industrial Sirens**: 3 long continuous siren blasts from industrial park command centers signal an off-site chemical or hazmat alert.
- **Odors & Plumes**: Heavy chemical fumes, chlorine odors, ammonia gas, or unusual colored smoke plumes emanating from industrial zones.

## 2. Shelter-in-Place Protocol (Inside Residential Areas)
- **Go Indoors Immediately**: If living in nearby barangays (Don Jose, Pulong Santa Cruz, Malitlit, Dita, Santo Domingo), seek indoor shelter immediately.
- **Seal Enclosures**: Close all windows, doors, and exhaust vents. Turn off central air intake and window air conditioners.
- **Damp Towel Barriers**: Place wet towels under door gaps and over window seams to block chemical vapor infiltration.
- **Breathe Through Damp Cloths**: Cover nose and mouth with a wet cloth if chemical odors penetrate indoors.

## 3. Evacuation Direction (Upwind & Crosswind Movement)
- **Check Wind Direction**: Observe flags, smoke, or trees to identify wind direction. Always move **UPWIND** or **CROSSWIND** (perpendicular to wind flow) away from the chemical plume.
- **Do NOT Evacuate Downwind**: Moving downwind puts you directly in the path of expanding gas clouds.

## 4. Emergency Decontamination & Reporting
- **Skin Exposure**: Flush exposed skin with clean running water for at least 15 minutes. Remove contaminated clothing immediately and seal in plastic bags.
- **Eye Exposure**: Rinse eyes thoroughly with clean water or saline for 15 minutes.
- **Report Incident**: Immediately notify Santa Rosa BFP Hazmat Unit & CDRRMO Command via AGAP SOS or hotlines.

> **Industrial Hub**: Santa Rosa hosts Laguna Technopark (LTIB), Greenfield Automotive Park, and Santa Rosa Commercial Complex.

## 1. Recognizing a Hazmat Alert
- 3 long continuous siren blasts from industrial park command centers signal an off-site chemical alert.
- Heavy chemical fumes, chlorine or ammonia odor, or unusual colored smoke plumes from industrial zones.

## 2. Shelter-in-Place (Nearby Barangays)
- Go indoors immediately if you live in Don Jose, Pulong Santa Cruz, Malitlit, Dita, or Santo Domingo.
- Close windows, doors, and exhaust vents; turn off central air intake and window ACs.
- Place wet towels under door gaps and over window seams.
- Breathe through a wet cloth if chemical odors penetrate indoors.

## 3. Evacuation Direction
- Check wind direction via flags, smoke, or trees. Move UPWIND or CROSSWIND, away from the plume.
- Do NOT evacuate downwind — you would walk into the expanding gas cloud.

## 4. Decontamination & Reporting
- Flush exposed skin with running water for 15 minutes; remove contaminated clothing and seal in plastic bags.
- Rinse eyes with clean water or saline for 15 minutes.
- Report via AGAP SOS or hotlines to Santa Rosa BFP Hazmat Unit & CDRRMO Command.

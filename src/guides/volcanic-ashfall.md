> **Regional Risk**: Santa Rosa is ~35km north-northwest of Taal Volcano; southerly winds can dump heavy ashfall on the city.

## 1. Personal Protection
- Wear N95 or KN95 respirators outdoors to filter silica ash. No mask? Use a damp cloth or handkerchief.
- Wear goggles or glasses; avoid contact lenses (ash causes corneal abrasions).
- Wear full-coverage clothing to prevent skin irritation from acidic ash.

## 2. Home & Water Protection
- Close all windows, doors, and air vents; place damp towels along door gaps.
- Cover open water drums, tanks, and deep wells against acidic contamination.
- Turn off split-type and window AC units — ash abrades cooling coils.

## 3. Roof Ash Removal
- Clear roofs promptly once ashfall subsides — wet ash is heavy enough to collapse roofs.
- Wear non-slip shoes and safety lines when clearing roof sheets.
- Spray ash lightly with water mist before sweeping to avoid raising dust.

## 4. Vehicles & Driving
- Avoid travel — ash cuts visibility to near zero and roads become ice-slick.
- Clean car air intake filters after driving through ashfall areas.
